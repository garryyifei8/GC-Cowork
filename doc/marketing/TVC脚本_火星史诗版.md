# "The Mars Frontier"
**An Epic Hollywood Blockbuster TVC Script & Storyboard**

**CLIENT:** Digit Space Singapore
**PRODUCT:** Avocado Cowork
**LENGTH:** 60-90 seconds
**STYLE:** Epic Sci-Fi, Christopher Nolan / Hans Zimmer vibe. Deep, existential, and sweeping. 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio.

### **THEME & VOICE**
*   **Narrator:** A grizzled, philosophical, and determined male voice (Brad Pitt style, similar to *Ad Astra*). 
*   **Music:** A sweeping, deep cinematic drone that builds into a massive, triumphant orchestral climax.

---

### **ACT I - THE PREPARATION (0:00 - 0:20)**

**[Title Card - Silent/Subtle]**
Fades in slowly: **"Avocado Cowork"** in elegant cinematic font over a black screen.

**[Visuals]**
*   **Scene 1 (Designer):** Close-up of **Avocado Designer**. Her eyes are focused, reflecting glowing holographic blueprints of a colossal Starship engine. The lighting is moody and blue.
*   **Scene 2 (Coder & Builder):** **Avocado Coder** is rapidly typing lines of launch sequence code, sweat on his green brow. Cut to **Avocado Builder**, wielding a massive hydro-spanner, tightening the final bolts on the rocket's golden heat shield.
*   **Scene 3 (HR & PM):** **Avocado HR** is analyzing the crew's vital signs on a floating monitor. **Avocado PM** stands at the absolute center of Mission Control, confidently swiping through the Avocado Cowork dashboard, syncing every single moving part perfectly.

**[Audio - Brad Pitt Voiceover]**
*(Deep, breathy, contemplative)*
"They say the stars are out of reach. That the vacuum of space... is no place for life."
*(Brief pause, music swells slightly)*
"But they underestimate the power... of absolute, unified focus."

---

### **ACT II - IGNITION (0:20 - 0:40)**

**[Visuals]**
*   **Scene 4 (CEO):** **Avocado CEO** stands solemnly in the commander's chair of the Starship. He puts on his sleek astronaut helmet. He nods slowly. He presses the glowing main ignition button. 
*   **Scene 5 (The Launch):** Extreme cinematic wide shot. A gigantic, stainless-steel Starship erupts with blinding plasma fire from its raptor engines. The camera shakes violently as it lifts off the pad in extreme slow-motion, piercing through the earth's atmosphere.

**[Audio - Brad Pitt Voiceover]**
*(Voice gets firmer, more intense)*
"To move mountains... to break the very bonds of gravity... you cannot act alone. You move as one mind. One vessel. One perfect system."
*(Rocket roaring SFX intertwines with the heavy bass)*

---

### **ACT III - THE FRONTIER (0:40 - 0:55)**

**[Visuals]**
*   **Scene 6 (The Journey):** The Starship breaks through the atmosphere and enters the silent, pitch-black void of space. A beautiful lens flare off the sun. 
*   **Scene 7 (Mars Base):** The ship lands gracefully on the dusty red surface of Mars. The six heroic Avocados step out onto the alien soil, standing side-by-side. Behind them, a massive, hyper-advanced Martian colony base is already under construction. A flag is planted: The Digit Space Singapore emblem.

**[Audio - Brad Pitt Voiceover]**
*(Calm, triumphant, awe-inspiring)*
"We don't just dream of the future. We build it... together."
