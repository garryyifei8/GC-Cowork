# "The Great Synergy"
**A Cute 3D Pixar-Style Animated TVC Script**

**CLIENT:** Digit Space Singapore
**PRODUCT:** Avocado Cowork
**LENGTH:** 60 seconds
**STYLE:** 3D Pixar Animation. Bright, colorful, highly energetic, and heartwarming. 16:9 aspect ratio.

### **THEME & VOICE**
*   **Narrator:** Cheerful, upbeat, and energetic (like a friendly Pixar narrator).
*   **Music:** A bright, bouncy marimba and Pizzicato strings track. Very joyful and comedic.

---

### **ACT I - THE OVERWHELM (0:00 - 0:15)**

**[Text On Screen]**
A colorful, bouncing 3D logo appears: **"Avocado Cowork"**

**[Visuals]**
Inside a bright, futuristic, yet messy office space. A cute, round, cartoonish green avocado character wearing oversized glasses is trying to carry a massive, teetering stack of glowing blueprints. He trips over a rogue coffee cup! The papers fly everywhere in slow motion. Other adorable avocado coworkers look panicked, running around in a chaotic rush.

**[Audio - Upbeat Narrator]**
"Running a team can sometimes feel like... a total spill!"
*(Comedic slip-and-fall sound effect!)*

---

### **ACT II - THE MAGIC TOUCH (0:15 - 0:30)**

**[Visuals]**
Suddenly, a confident "Manager Avocado" enters. She holds up a glowing, magical tablet running the Avocado Cowork platform. She taps the screen. A beautiful, sparkling green holographic wave ripples across the chaotic office. The flying papers magically organize themselves and gently float down onto neat glowing desks. The chaotic avocados stop and gasp in awe.

**[Audio - Narrator]**
"But what if you had a touch of magic? Meet Avocado Cowork."
*(Magical sparkling chime sound effect)*

---

### **ACT III - PERFECT HARMONY (0:30 - 0:45)**

**[Visuals]**
The office is now beautifully lit and peaceful. The adorable avocados are passing holographic task cards to each other with perfect robotic precision. It's like a joyful, synchronized dance. They high-five each other. They assemble a shiny, toy-like futuristic Starship model on the center table in record time, cheering happily.

**[Audio - Narrator]**
"Turn office chaos into pure synergy. Sync tasks, spark joy, and build amazing things together."
*(Bouncy, joyful music builds up)*

---

### **ACT IV - THE LAUNCH (0:45 - 1:00)**

**[Visuals]**
The toy Starship model magically ignites with a tiny blue spark and launches around the room, trailing rainbow stardust. The avocado team jumps for joy, hugging each other. The camera tracks the toy ship as it flies toward the screen.
The screen transitions to a beautiful, soft, colorful 3D title card.

**[Audio - Narrator]**
"Avocado Cowork. Your team's superpower!"

**[Text On Screen]**
**Avocado Cowork**
A Masterpiece by Digit Space Singapore

**[End]**
