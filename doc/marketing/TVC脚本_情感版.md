# "The Last Seed / 最后一颗种子"
**An Emotional & Sentimental 3D Animated TVC**

**CLIENT:** Digital Space Singapore
**PRODUCT:** Avocado Cowork
**LENGTH:** 60-90 seconds
**STYLE:** 3D Pixar-style Animation (Emotional, Cinematic, Touching). Soft warm lighting contrasting with cold environments.

---

### **ACT I - THE DARKEST HOUR (0:00 - 0:20)**

**[Visual]**
A cold, barren, and wind-swept alien planet. Snow and ash are falling. The remains of a crashed Starship sit in the background. 
Inside a makeshift, freezing tent, the six adorable cartoon avocado characters look exhausted, bruised, and on the verge of giving up. 
In the center of them lies a tiny, faintly glowing green "Core Seed" (representing their collective dream/project). The glow is flickering, almost dying.

**[Audio]**
*SFX:* Howling cold wind. 
*Music:* A slow, melancholic, and lonely piano melody.

**[Action]**
- **CEO Avocado** is desperately trying to shield the seed with his tiny hands, shivering.
- **PM Avocado** drops his cracked data-pad, burying his face in his hands in defeat.
- **Designer, Coder, Builder, and Admin** sit in the shadows, looking at the dying light with tearful, helpless eyes. The light flickers and dims even more. It feels like the end.

### **ACT II - IGNITING HOPE (0:20 - 0:45)**

**[Visual]**
Just as the light is about to completely extinguish, **Coder Avocado** slowly stands up. He takes out his very last glowing code-battery and plugs it into the center console.
Seeing this, the others begin to move.

**[Action]**
- **Builder Avocado** wipes his tears, takes out his heavy wrench, and quickly dismantles a piece of scrap metal to build a protective dome over the seed.
- **Designer Avocado** picks up a shard of broken glass, adjusting the angle to reflect the faint starlight from outside directly onto the seed.
- **Admin & PM Avocados** step forward, pulling everyone into a tight circle. 
- They all place their tiny green hands together on the Avocado Cowork terminal. A warm, golden UI interface lights up, connecting their vital signs and energy.

**[Audio]**
*Music:* The piano melody begins to swell, joined by warm, emotional cello and soaring strings. The tempo builds with hope.

### **ACT III - THE BLOOM OF SYNERGY (0:45 - 1:10)**

**[Visual]**
Through their combined effort and the Avocado Cowork platform synchronizing their actions, the tiny "Core Seed" absorbs their collective warmth. 
*Ba-bump.* The seed pulses like a heartbeat. 
Suddenly, a brilliant, warm, magical golden-green light explodes softly from the seed! 
A beautiful, massive, glowing Tree of Life rapidly sprouts from the ground, shattering the cold tent, its glowing leaves melting the snow and illuminating the entire dark planet into a vibrant, warm paradise.

**[Action]**
The six avocados let go of the console and look up. The warm light reflects in their large, tear-filled eyes. They are no longer cold. **CEO Avocado** looks back at his team, shedding a joyful tear, and they all pull in for a massive, tight, emotional group hug.

**[Audio]**
*Music:* A triumphant, deeply moving orchestral climax that touches the soul.
*Voiceover (Warm, gentle, and reassuring female voice):* 
"Even in the coldest winter, we are never alone. When our strengths connect, miracles take root." 
*(“即使在最寒冷的凛冬，我们也从不孤单。当力量相连，奇迹便会生根发芽。”)*

### **ACT IV - OUTRO (1:10 - 1:20)**

**[Visual]**
The camera slowly pulls back into the starry sky, framing the beautiful glowing tree on the now-living planet. 
The stars align to form the elegant 3D text: **DIGITAL SPACE SINGAPORE**.
Below it, a softer, warm glowing logo appears.

**[Audio]**
*Voiceover:* 
"Avocado Cowork. Grow together, succeed together."
*(“Avocado Cowork. 共同成长，彼此成就。”)*
